## Recurring Formats

### Throwback Thursday (every Thursday)
- Celebrity drama/feuds from 2+ years ago
- Focus on stories people still Google
- Good era: 2020–2024 iconic moments
- Title format: "Throwback: [Story]" or "#TBT: [Story]"

### Top X Tuesday (every Tuesday)
- Vary the number (Top 5, Top 7, Top 10, Top 3)
- Include year or "of all time" in title for SEO
- Keep it sassy and shareable
