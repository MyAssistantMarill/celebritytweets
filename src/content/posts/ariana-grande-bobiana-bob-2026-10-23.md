Fourteen years. That's how long Ariana Grande wore her signature sky-high ponytail like a crown. And then, with zero warning and a single heart emoji caption, she posted a series of Instagram selfies on October 22nd rocking a retro-inspired, chin-skimming bob — and Twitter hasn't recovered since.

The look features side-swept bangs, flipped-out ends, and soft chestnut brown color. Vogue immediately christened it the "Bobiana Grande" era. Two million likes in under 24 hours. The internet said *yes*.
