Few celebrity feuds have played out as publicly — and as dramatically — on social media as the ongoing saga between Taylor Swift and Kanye West. What started at an awards show turned into years of tweets, diss tracks, and internet meltdowns. Let's break it all down.

## How It Started

It all kicked off at the 2009 MTV Video Music Awards when Kanye famously interrupted Taylor's acceptance speech. The moment became a cultural flashpoint — and things only escalated from there.

## The Tweets That Shook the Internet

The feud went fully online when Kanye released "Famous" in 2016, featuring a lyric about Taylor. What followed was a back-and-forth on Twitter that had the entire internet picking sides.

Kanye's camp responded with leaked phone recordings, which Taylor's team called "manipulated." The debate over who was telling the truth dominated entertainment news for weeks.

## Where Things Stand Now

Since then, Taylor released *Reputation* — widely seen as her response album — while Kanye has gone through a very public personal journey. The two have largely avoided directly referencing each other in recent years, but the internet has never forgotten.

## The Takeaway

Love them or hate them, Taylor and Kanye's feud defined an era of celebrity social media drama. It showed just how powerful — and destructive — Twitter could be when two of the world's biggest stars go to war on it.
